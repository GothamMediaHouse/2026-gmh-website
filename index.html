WHO WE ARE

Built around nearly two decades behind the camera.

Gotham Media House is led by Matt Fox, an Emmy Award–winning filmmaker whose work spans drama, documentary, corporate, and live broadcast — including major national broadcasts, presidential debates, and large-scale live music events. Every story deserves to be told with authenticity and impact, and that starts with a hands-on approach from first call to final delivery.

Nineteen years in, that hands-on approach covers the full technical range — directing, cinematography, audio, lighting, and live event engineering — behind brand films, live streams, and cinematic documentaries for clients across a range of industries.
